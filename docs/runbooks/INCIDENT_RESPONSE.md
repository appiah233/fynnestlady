# Incident Response Runbook

1. Confirm and classify impact without expanding access.
2. Notify Eric Appiah Owusu and identify an incident coordinator.
3. Contain safely: protect customers, preserve evidence, rotate exposed credentials, and pause affected capability when necessary.
4. Record UTC chronology, affected revision/environment, symptoms, decisions, and evidence without sensitive values.
5. Recover using tested rollback or forward repair; reconcile payments, orders, and inventory explicitly.
6. Verify customer/security impact and communication obligations with the owner.
7. Conduct a blameless review and track remediation to closure.

Never delete evidence, conceal impact, or announce resolution before verification.
