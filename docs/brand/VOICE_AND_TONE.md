# Voice and Tone

## Voice

Knowledgeable without sounding clinical. Confident without exaggeration. Personal without being intrusive. Premium without sounding distant. Supportive without diagnosing. Educational without lecturing. Commercial without aggressively pushing products.

## Writing Principles

- Use plain, specific language and explain product purpose.
- Say what is known, omit what is not, and label what requires verification.
- Prefer “explore,” “may help support,” and “designed for” where verified.
- Encourage patch testing or professional advice only when supported by authoritative product guidance.
- Separate cosmetic concern language from medical-condition language.
- Never use fear, shame, guaranteed outcomes, false scarcity, or invented authority.

## Context

- **Discovery:** curious, supportive, low pressure.
- **Product pages:** precise, complete, evidence-bound.
- **Checkout:** calm, concise, transparent.
- **Errors:** direct, accountable, recovery-focused.
- **Transactional messages:** factual, reassuring, privacy-conscious.
- **Education:** structured, source-aware, never diagnostic.

Content involving ingredients, warnings, contraindications, clinical evidence, or efficacy requires human verification against trustworthy source material.
