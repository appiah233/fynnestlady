# Content Guidelines

## Source of Truth

Only publish product facts supplied by the manufacturer, verified catalogue records, or approved authoritative sources. Record provenance and review state for regulated or safety-relevant content.

## Required Product Content

Show only available verified fields: identity, genuine variant and size, price, availability, purpose, relevant concerns, ingredients, use instructions, routine position, warnings, delivery information, and FAQs. Missing data stays absent or blocks publication; filler is prohibited.

## Claims

- No cure, guaranteed removal, overnight transformation, medically guaranteed result, or universal suitability claims.
- No diagnostic language or inference of medical conditions.
- A product-concern link means “may be explored in relation to this concern.”
- Mark uncertain claims `HUMAN CONTENT VERIFICATION REQUIRED` and keep them unpublished.

## Editorial Quality

Use descriptive headings, short paragraphs, meaningful link text, accessible image alternatives, consistent units, and transparent commercial language. Distinguish product facts from education and customer reviews.

## Governance

Material brand claims require owner approval. Safety, ingredient, efficacy, and medical-adjacent statements require ProductAgent and SecurityAgent review plus human verification.
