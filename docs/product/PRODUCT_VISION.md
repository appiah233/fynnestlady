# Product Vision

Fynnestlady Skincare will be a premium, trustworthy e-commerce experience that helps customers move from a skincare need or goal to understanding options, exploring relevant products, learning purpose and use, making an informed decision, purchasing safely, and tracking fulfilment.

## Business Goals

- Sell skincare and body-care products through a complete, reliable commerce journey.
- Translate 12 years of experience into useful guidance and customer confidence.
- Build trust through verified content, transparent pricing, and dependable fulfilment.
- Give administrators safe control of catalogue, inventory, content, orders, and promotions.

## Customer Outcomes

Customers can discover products by catalogue structure or stated concern, understand verified product information, complete checkout without surprises, and manage orders and account information securely.

## Launch Goals

Deliver the bounded MVP in `MVP_SCOPE.md`, pass security/accessibility/commerce release gates, and provide operational evidence for backup, rollback, payment reconciliation, and order fulfilment.

## Principles

Guided rather than aggressively sold to; informative rather than diagnostic; mobile-first; accessible; server-authoritative; evidence-bound; operationally supportable.
