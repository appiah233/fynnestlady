# Product Requirements

## Functional Requirements

- Customers can browse, search, filter, inspect verified product details, choose genuine variants, maintain a cart, checkout, pay, and track orders.
- Customers can securely register, authenticate, reset passwords, and manage profile, addresses, and order history.
- Administrators can manage catalogue, taxonomy, content, media, prices, stock, publishing, orders, fulfilment, discounts, and audit history according to role.
- Concern-led discovery is administrator-managed many-to-many taxonomy and communicates exploration, not treatment.
- Educational content supports guides and FAQs with provenance and review state.

## Quality Requirements

- WCAG 2.2 AA target, keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic controls, reduced-motion respect, and representative responsive verification.
- Server-authoritative commerce, precise money, controlled state machines, immutable order snapshots, concurrency-safe inventory, and idempotent payment handling.
- Secure defaults, least privilege, validated input, protected secrets, auditable privileged actions, and privacy-conscious logs.
- Observable health, structured errors, backup/restore evidence, rollback, and performance budgets defined before implementation.

## Content Requirements

No fabricated product fields or medical diagnosis. Product publication requires verified mandatory data. Unverified skincare claims remain unpublished and visibly flagged for human review.
