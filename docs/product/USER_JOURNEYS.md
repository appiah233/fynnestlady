# User Journeys

## Concern-led Discovery

1. Customer states a skincare goal or selects a managed concern.
2. The experience explains the concern scope without diagnosis.
3. Relevant products are presented with transparent filters.
4. Product purpose, use, ingredients, warnings, and suitability information appear only when verified.
5. Customer makes an informed choice or leaves without pressure.

## Purchase and Account

1. Browse or search and select a genuine variant.
2. Add to persistent cart and review availability and server-calculated totals.
3. Authenticate or continue through the approved checkout mode.
4. Provide delivery address and confirm the final total.
5. Complete Paystack payment; server verifies it independently.
6. Receive order confirmation and view status/history in the account.

## Administrator Catalogue to Fulfilment

1. Authenticate with authorized admin role.
2. Create a draft product, genuine variants, verified content, media, concern links, price, and inventory.
3. Resolve validation/content-review issues and publish.
4. Receive a verified paid order and process it through controlled states.
5. Fulfil, record shipment information, notify the customer, and retain audit history.

## Failure and Recovery

Payment pending, stock conflict, invalid discount, upload rejection, session expiry, and delivery-validation failures must preserve customer input where safe, explain the state, avoid duplicate charges/orders, and offer a clear recovery path.
