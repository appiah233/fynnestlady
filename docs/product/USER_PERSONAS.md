# User Personas

## Goal-led Customer

Wants help finding products related to a stated cosmetic concern without being diagnosed. Needs plain explanations, relevant filters, verified use guidance, realistic language, mobile usability, and trust signals.

## Routine-focused Customer

Knows their routine or product type and wants efficient search, comparison, genuine sizes/variants, ingredients, availability, delivery clarity, and repeat purchasing.

## Cautious First-time Buyer

Needs evidence of experience, transparent policies, secure payment, clear errors, accessible support, and no high-pressure sales tactics.

## Store Administrator

Manages verified catalogue data, stock, imagery, taxonomy, content, prices, promotions, orders, and fulfilment. Needs least-privilege access, validation, audit history, safe workflows, and explicit publish states.

## Owner

Needs commercial oversight, trustworthy reporting, controlled releases, auditable decisions, operational resilience, and exclusive authority over merges, risk acceptance, and production deployment.
