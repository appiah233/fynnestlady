# ADR-013: Skincare Concern Taxonomy

Status: **Proposed**

## Context
Concern-led discovery is central, but the real catalogue and verified terminology are not yet available.
## Problem
Support useful discovery without diagnosis or prematurely hardcoded claims.
## Options
Hardcoded concerns; administrator-managed taxonomy; free-form tags.
## Decision
Use managed Concern records with reviewed copy and many-to-many product associations.
## Consequences
Taxonomy can evolve with catalogue evidence; governance and content review are required.
## Risks
Associations may imply treatment if copy is careless or unverified.
## Alternatives
Hardcoding creates migration debt; free-form tags become inconsistent and unsafe.
## Revisit conditions
Verified catalogue/content research establishes hierarchy, synonyms, or localization needs.
